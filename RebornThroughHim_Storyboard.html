THROUGH HIM

Brand Film — Storyboard

Runtime: ~45s  |  Format: Vertical (IG Reels / TikTok)  |  Location: St Anne's Park

DANIEL

Lead. The one who chooses faith.

FRIEND 2

Supporting. Part of the group.

FRIEND 3

Supporting. Part of the group.

SCENE 1

Walk & Sit

00:00 – 00:05

Direction

3 friends walking through the park, easy energy. They reach a bench and sit. A laugh, a shove, someone glancing at their phone. Establishes the group as a unit before the split happens.

Wide-ish tracking shot as they walk in, settle on the bench. Keep this fast: it's setup, not story.

No dialogue — body language and energy carry it.

SCENE 2

The Conversation Turns

00:05 – 00:16

Direction

Daniel's last line lands flat and certain — not defensive, not a joke. That stillness after it is the beat. Camera tightens slowly on his face as the friends' voices start to drop under the push-in into Scene 3.

FRIEND 2: "You guys still coming to the party tonight, yeah?"
FRIEND 3: "Of course bro, I can't wait."
DANIEL: "Nah, I'm going to read my Bible tonight. I've got church in the morning."
FRIEND 3: "Bro, what happened to the old fun you?"
FRIEND 2: "What's next, you're gonna start a Christian clothing brand in Ireland?"
DANIEL: "Yeah. That's what I want to do."

SCENE 3

VFX Beat: The Blink

00:16 – 00:28

Direction

Camera close on Daniel's face. He blinks. Friends are gone. He's alone on the bench. Light has dropped. Beat of stillness: no dialogue.

The bench dips slightly beside him. A soft golden light appears — contained, warm, weight without form. Human-adjacent but never resolved. One dove flies past in 3D VFX; the light follows the direction the dove flew. He blinks again: back — friends still there, still talking, like no time has passed.

No dialogue in this whole sequence. Sound design carries it: ambient park noise drops out almost completely, comes back with the blink-back.

No dialogue — entirely visual. Sound design carries it.

SCENE 4

The Group Leaves

00:28 – 00:38

Direction

Group gets up to head off. A look back at Daniel: half a question, no words needed. Group walks off. Daniel starts to rise to follow, out of habit. Flash-cut, half a second: the golden light beside him on the bench. He stops.

One line is enough. The flashback interrupting his instinct to follow is the real beat — keep it quick, don't let it drag.

FRIEND 2: "Come on, let's go."

SCENE 5

The Turn

00:38 – 00:45

Direction

Daniel turns the other way: the direction the light followed the dove. Walks off alone, settled, not sad. Friends continue on in the background, oblivious. Outro music comes in here — carries through to the end card.

No dialogue — Outro music carries through to end card.
